# Sprint S6 — Listings, Campaign og scoped mæglerdeltagelse

**Status:** Planlagt · listing/feed og Campaign har separate juridiske gates  
**Mål:** Importere licenserede udbud, vise relevante muligheder og give mæglere en lovlig, afgrænset distributions- og udførerrolle — uden M3-lead- eller invitationsmodellen.

## Scope A — Listings/feed
- Source registry and license reference.
- Versioned listing data and status.
- Feed adapters (API/SFTP/webhook) with checkpoint, idempotency and quarantine.
- Address resolution and attribution.
- Matching against buyer orders.
- SOLD/WITHDRAWN/STALE and license expiry behavior.

## Scope B — Campaign
- Versioned purpose-specific marketing consent history.
- Campaign creation by verified agent organization for its own active licensed listing.
- Audience estimate with privacy controls.
- Content review/scanning, scheduling and delivery through Optata inbox.
- Relevance, quota, mute, suppression, frequency and quiet-hour controls.
- Campaign metrics and stop controls.

## Scope C — scoped participation
- Organization identity/CVR verification.
- Participation license bound to organization, territory, mandate and task.
- Access enforcement in API and RLS.
- Audit and revocation.

**Ude:** M3 invitation/lead relay; buyer→agent contact reveal; free agent dialogue; CURIOS; campaign delivery to non-consenting buyers; audience export; listing scraping.

## Consent data rule
Do not use one mutable row as the consent record. Store immutable consent events with purpose, scope, text_version, legal_basis_ref, actor, timestamp, source and supersession/revocation reference. A current-state projection may be maintained for fast checks, but must be rebuildable from the event history.

## Delivery algorithm (normative order)
1. Verify campaign approved and listing active/licensed/owned by advertiser.
2. Recompute eligible audience from current orders and current consent.
3. Apply mute, suppression, revocation, frequency and quiet-hour exclusions.
4. Apply relevance threshold.
5. Apply audience floor and k-anonymity to the final eligible set and every exposed aggregate cell.
6. Recheck eligibility and consent at actual send time.
7. Deliver only to Optata inbox; record minimum necessary audit/metrics.
8. Charge quota only for successful delivery; stop immediately on license/status/consent failure.

Audience floor and k≥5 are privacy safeguards, not legal permission to profile or market. L43/L44 must approve the targeting and controller/processor roles.

## Acceptkriterier
- No import, display or campaign without active license_ref and valid source terms.
- Feed replay is idempotent; invalid records quarantined; no silent drops.
- STALE is not SOLD; only explicit source evidence may mark SOLD.
- Listing changes preserve bitemporal history and source provenance.
- Consent can be granted, revoked and granted again; complete history survives.
- Revocation before delivery prevents send even if audience was estimated/approved earlier.
- Final audience is recalculated after every exclusion; advertiser sees counts only, never identities/pseudonyms.
- k and suppression checks cover all exposed cells and time-window/differencing risk.
- Mute, suppression and frequency limits are enforced in the domain at send time.
- Mute is permanent unless buyer explicitly unmutes; new consent does not silently undo mute.
- Agent can access only organization∩territory∩mandate∩task and only while participation license is active.
- Listing sold/withdrawn/stale or license revoked stops relevant campaign/matching.
- No external contact route or buyer reveal is created by M3.
