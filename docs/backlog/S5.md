# Sprint S5 — OfferLedger og budprocessen

**Status:** Design/planlagt · produktionsimplementering blokeret af LG-1, LG-2, LG-5 og LG-7  
**Mål:** Gennemføre en struktureret, auditerbar bud- og modbudsproces på et aktivt SaleMandate uden at gøre fri chat eller dataeksport til transaktionsmekanisme.

## Scope
- Offer aggregate and append-only event ledger.
- Buyer eligibility: VERIFIED + unexpired evidence.
- Mandate validity, seller parties, executor scope and version checks.
- Structured offer terms, financing status, expiry and counter-offer chain.
- Accept/decline/withdraw/expire commands.
- Limited contextual message field only if legally approved and technically constrained.
- Fulfillment handoff to approved partner; no custody of purchase funds.

**Ude:** gammel fri relay-chat; redaction layers as primary transaction design; generic `deal` aggregate; paid reveal as a legal shortcut; automatic sale package; escrow or custody of purchase price.

## Core model
Offer is immutable. Every action creates an `offer_event`: SUBMITTED, ACKNOWLEDGED, COUNTERED, ACCEPTED, DECLINED, WITHDRAWN, EXPIRED. Counter-offer creates a new offer linked to `parent_offer_id`; it does not mutate the previous offer. The accepted offer references the exact active mandate version and parties.

## Acceptkriterier
- Offer command rejects absent, suspended, revoked, expired or wrong-version mandate.
- Offer command rejects non-VERIFIED buyer or expired/revoked attestation.
- Offer terms, parent reference, expiry, financing status, actor and mandate version are immutable.
- A counter-offer creates a new record/event and preserves the full chain.
- Duplicate idempotency key returns the original result; conflicting replay is rejected.
- Acceptance rechecks all parties, mandate state, offer state, evidence freshness and actor permissions inside one transaction.
- No UI/API response describes platform acceptance as a binding purchase agreement unless legal opinion and contract model explicitly permit that claim.
- No purchase price is received, held, transferred or escrowed by Optata.
- Partner handoff includes only approved minimum data; no full orderbook or unrelated message history.
- Race tests prove one valid terminal transition; audit precedes side effect.

## Release gates
LG-1 and LG-5 must be closed for the platform's role and offer/acceptance process. LG-7 must be closed before any fee/payment related to transaction. Partner/fulfillment model and contract templates must be approved.

## Definition of Done
Append-only ledger proven by DB permissions and tests; all commands idempotent; concurrency/TOCTOU tested; legal wording approved; partner handoff tested; no escrow path; signed human review.
